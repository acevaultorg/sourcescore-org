## Bundle 🅶 — Source Score (the new addition spec)

**Why this bundle works** (correcting my earlier "skip all 4" framing):

- The legacy citation-formatter sub-niche (EasyBib/Citation Machine) IS saturated. My earlier read.
- The **AI-content-era citation/source-quality** sub-niche is GENUINELY emerging, low-incumbent, high-LLM-citation-moat. Different market.

**4-domain architecture:**

1. **sourcescore.org** = parent brand. Universal source-quality scorer. Paste any URL → 0–100 quality score combining DR + age + author E-E-A-T + cite-count + claim-coverage + (vertical-specific overlay: media-bias for news, NCHFP-cite for recipes, npm-score for code).
2. **citingscore.org** redirects to sourcescore.org/ai-verify — the AI-Citation Verifier (highest individual APS in the bundle: 32). Paste LLM output → tool detects citations, verifies they exist, checks claim support.
3. **citingsources.org** redirects to sourcescore.org/citing-sources — Modern Citation Reference. Programmatic 7 styles × 30 source-types = ~200 unique pages covering modern source types (AI outputs, repos, podcasts, datasets) where Purdue OWL has gaps.
4. **citescore.org** redirects to sourcescore.org/cite-track — Open Cite Tracker (OpenAlex/Crossref alt to Elsevier's paywalled CiteScore). ⚠ Trademark-careful framing required.

**Bundle compound:** Σ individual APS (28+32+25+18) = 103 × ×1.20 stacking = **124**.

**Differentiator vs. NewsGuard / Ad Fontes / Purdue OWL / Elsevier:**
- NewsGuard: news-only + paywalled. SourceScore: cross-domain + free.
- Ad Fontes Media: news-only.
- Purdue OWL: legacy source types only. SourceScore: modern (AI-era) source types.
- Elsevier CiteScore: paywalled, journal-only. SourceScore Cite-Track: free, OpenAlex 250k journals.

**Ship sequence:** anchor sourcescore.org first → ship the AI-Citation Verifier (highest-APS sub-tool) within ~2 brain-days as lead product → observe 30d SERP signal → if ramping, scaffold the other 3 sub-tools. **If verifier doesn't ramp, you only spent €17 on 2 domains, not €34 on 4.**
